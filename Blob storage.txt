When to use blob storage:

1. Dealing with huge amount of unstructured data like images, videos etc.
2. You upload data to a blob and get back a url,you can use this url later to download the data
3. They often work in conjunction with CDNs to enable faster downloads.
4. In a typical setup you will have a core database like Postgres or DynamoDB that has pointers (just a url) to the blobs stored in S3.
5. Adv: they are highly durable and highly scalable, cost efffective

Ex: Amazon S3, Azure blob etc.
