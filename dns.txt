CNAME: alias for a different Subdomain
how will it be used: suppose you want to direct shop.mywebsite.com traffc to amazon.com 
you can configure a cname for shop.mywebsite.com as amazon.com , so dns lok up will take you
to amazon.com even though you trigger shop.mywebsite.com

step 1: you did a dns lookup for rr1---sn-ci5gup-pmcs.googlevideo.com
step 2: dns server replies that this is a cname/alias for rr1.sn-ci5gup-pmcs.googlevideo.com

step 4: you get the A record for rr1.sn-ci5gup-pmcs.googlevideo.com
