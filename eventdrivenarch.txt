EDA:
This involves publishing events and some consumer listening to them and acting on top of it.
This involves Message queues (kafka) and sometimes event streams (kafka streams)

When to use:
1. When dealing with financial data or Audit requirements.
2. When you need realtime data pipelines (or aggregations or insights) <1min .
This uses EDA with streams and stream processing (flink)
3. Need to replay/ reprocess historical data.
